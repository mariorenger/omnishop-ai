# ADR-000: Template

> Copy this file for each new decision. Status: `Proposed` → `Accepted` →
> (`Superseded by ADR-NNN`). Keep ADRs short and honest about trade-offs.

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** (roles)
- **Supersedes / Superseded by:** —

## Context

What forces are at play? Constraints (business, license, cost, team, timeline)?
What problem must this decision solve, and what must it *not* break?

## Options

List the realistic options (not strawmen). For each: what it is, and its
license/cost/lock-in posture.

## Evaluation

Compare options against the criteria that matter here (license/SaaS-suitability,
maturity, cost at scale, extensibility, migration difficulty, lock-in).

## Decision

The choice, stated plainly.

## Why

The reasoning that makes the decision follow from the evaluation.

## Trade-offs

What we give up. What gets harder. What we are betting on.

## Migration path

How we'd replace this later if the bet is wrong (this is why we wrap it behind an
interface). What has to stay stable for the swap to be cheap.
